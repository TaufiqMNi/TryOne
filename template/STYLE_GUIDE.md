# Style guide edit (dari video rujukan pertama)

## Struktur
Hook soalan dalam 1s pertama ("Korang perasan tak...") → konteks (kad tajuk tarikh/peristiwa) → isu + bukti → kaitan masa kini → CTA ("cuba korang share").

## Editing
- Purata shot ~2.2s (≤2.5s). Jump cut + punch-in zoom pada host.
- Potong ke B-roll setiap kali sebut fakta, nama, tarikh, tempat.
- Peralihan flash putih / fade cepat. Montaj 3-4 shot laju pada titik penekanan.

## Framing & B-roll
- Host di tengah, medium shot paras dada, mikrofon podcast dalam frame, latar rak buku gelap dengan bokeh.
- B-roll: footage arkib, tangkapan skrin artikel (Ken Burns), ilustrasi 3D/AI simbolik.
- B-roll sebagai kad 4:5 berbucu bulat di atas hitam + vignette. Kredit sumber kecil di bawah.

## Warna
- Low-key sinematik, hitam di-crush, vignette kuat, kulit hangat.
- Palet: #070306, #181418, #322528, #662A34 (aksen), #5B5A58, #9A9691, #857270, #AAB8A8.
- Aksen: kuning #FFD400 (penekanan), emas #E0B04A (motion graphic, garisan bercahaya atas biru gelap).
- Arkib dinyahtepu + tekstur halftone halus.

## Kapsyen
- 2-4 perkataan, sans-serif bold putih + bayang lembut, tengah ~70% tinggi, Bahasa Melayu santai.
- Petikan Inggeris: kuning #FFD400, bold italic, satu perkataan sekali.
- Kad tajuk: condensed bold putih uppercase (Bebas Neue / Oswald).

## Audio
- VO jelas di atas, music bed tension/ambient perlahan sepanjang video.
- SFX whoosh/hit setiap potongan, riser sebelum pendedahan. Master ~-8 LUFS, tiada senyap.

## Nada
Serius, mendesak, mendidik, tapi santai ("korang", "dorang").

## Prompt tetap
```
Create a 9:16 vertical short-form video (60–90s, 30fps) in a documentary-essay
talking-head style about [TOPIK].
STRUCTURE: Hook question in first 1s → context with date/event title card → core
issue with evidence → link to present day → CTA ("cuba korang share").
HOST: Centered medium shot, chest-up, podcast mic in frame, dark bookshelf
background, shallow depth of field.
EDITING: Avg shot ~2s, jump cuts with punch-in zoom, cut to B-roll on every fact,
white flash / quick fade transitions, rapid montage on emphasis beats.
B-ROLL: Archival footage, article screenshots with Ken Burns, AI/3D symbolic
illustrations; rounded-corner 4:5 insets on black with vignette + source credit.
COLOR: Low-key cinematic, crushed blacks, vignette, warm skin, maroon shadows
(#322528, #070306), burgundy accent (#662A34); gold (#E0B04A) motion graphics.
CAPTIONS: 2–4 words, bold white sans with shadow at ~70% height, casual Malay;
English quotes yellow (#FFD400) bold italic one word at a time.
AUDIO: Clear VO, low tension music bed, whoosh/hit SFX on cuts, ~-8 LUFS.
TEMPLATE OVERLAY: Apply template/overlay_1080x1920.png (partner logos top, donation
banner bottom) on every frame. Captions must stay above the banner.
```
